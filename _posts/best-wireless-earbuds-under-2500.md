## Detailed Performance Breakdown: What to Look for in Budget TWS Earbuds

### Active Noise Cancellation (ANC)

ANC helps reduce background noise and improves the listening experience during travel, study sessions, and office work. In the sub-2500 segment, hybrid ANC implementations have improved drastically, with options like the Realme Buds T310 offering up to 46dB of noise suppression.

### Battery Life

For the best experience, choose earbuds that offer at least 35 hours of total playback. This ensures you only need to charge the case once a week under normal listening conditions.

### Gaming Performance

Low-latency modes below 60ms provide better synchronization between audio and visuals during gaming. This is critical for fast-paced shooters and reactive audio cues.

### Water Resistance

IP54 or higher protection is recommended for workouts, runs, and outdoor usage to protect your investment against sweat and light rain.

### Bluetooth Version

Bluetooth 5.4 provides improved stability, lower power consumption, and better dual-device connectivity.
