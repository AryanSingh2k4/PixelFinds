Planning a major life event in the USA? Whether you are expecting a new baby or tying the knot, creating a registry is a critical step to ensure you get exactly what you need while saving money. 

While there are many dedicated baby and wedding retailers, millions of Americans are turning to Amazon for their registry needs. Why? Because Amazon offers unmatched convenience, a massive product selection, and some of the best perks in the industry—including **hefty completion discounts, massive return windows, and universal registry tools**.

In this guide, we'll break down the incredible benefits of both the **Amazon Baby Registry** and the **Amazon Wedding Registry** for USA customers in 2026.

---

<h2 id="amazon-baby-registry">The Amazon Baby Registry: Everything You Need for Your Little One</h2>

Preparing for a new arrival is stressful enough without worrying about where to register. The [Amazon Baby Registry](https://amzn.to/4gcUDeK) is universally loved by parents for its ease of use and phenomenal perks. 

### Key Benefits & Perks for Parents

1. **The Famous Completion Discount**
   Once you are within 14 days of your expected due date, Amazon unlocks a completion discount for remaining eligible items on your registry. Standard customers get a 10% discount, but **Amazon Prime members get a generous 15% discount**. This is a fantastic way to buy those big-ticket items (like strollers or cribs) that weren't gifted to you.

2. **Free Welcome Box**
   Amazon rewards eligible registry creators with a free **Welcome Box** packed with samples and full-sized surprises for both parents and baby (valued at approximately $35). To claim it, you simply need to create the registry, complete a certain percentage of the checklist, and have a qualifying purchase of at least $10 made from your registry.

3. **365-Day Return Policy**
   Babies grow fast, and sometimes you receive items you don't end up needing. Amazon offers an incredible **365-day free return window** on most gifts purchased from your baby registry, giving you ultimate peace of mind.

4. **Universal Registry & Diaper Funds**
   Want a specific handmade item from Etsy or a boutique? Use Amazon's Universal Registry browser extension to add items from *any* website. Furthermore, you can enable a "Diaper Fund," allowing friends and family to contribute financially.

<h2 id="amazon-wedding-registry">The Amazon Wedding Registry: Building Your Dream Home Together</h2>

For engaged couples in the USA, the [Amazon Wedding Registry](https://amzn.to/4v5uD8P) provides a modern, flexible alternative to traditional department store registries. 

### Key Benefits & Perks for Couples

1. **20% Registry Completion Discount**
   This is arguably the best perk. After your wedding date, Amazon offers a **20% completion discount** on remaining eligible items in your registry. This discount can be applied to orders up to $1,500, meaning you can save up to **$300** on everything from stand mixers to vacuum cleaners. (Note: Items must be shipped and sold by Amazon).

2. **Unmatched Selection & Universal Registry**
   You aren't limited to fine china. Register for camping gear, electronics, smart home devices, or patio furniture. Plus, just like the Baby Registry, you can use the Universal Registry tool to add items from other websites, keeping all your preferences in one single, easy-to-find list.

3. **180-Day Returns**
   Wedding planning is chaotic, and dealing with returns shouldn't be. Amazon gives couples a full **180 days (6 months)** to return wedding gifts, providing ample time to settle in after the honeymoon.

4. **Group Gifting & Gift Card Funds**
   Got your eye on a $600 espresso machine? Enable **Group Gifting** so multiple guests can contribute whatever amount they prefer toward the same large item. If you aren't sure what you need yet, you can also set up a Gift Card Fund.

5. **Bonus Gifts from Brands**
   Depending on the brands you register for and what guests purchase, couples may be eligible for free bonus gifts directly from participating partners (like premium cookware brands).

[Start Your Free Amazon Wedding Registry Here](https://amzn.to/4v5uD8P)
